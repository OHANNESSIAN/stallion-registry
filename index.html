About StallionRegistry.com Editorial Scope

StallionRegistry.com is an independent international Thoroughbred stallion directory and bloodstock reference portal covering active sires and breeding farms across Kentucky, Florida, Ireland, England, and Canada.

Every listed sire profile highlights sire-line pedigree, black-type progeny achievements, progeny earnings standings, and verified standing farm locations to help broodmare owners and bloodstock advisors evaluate mating options quickly.

Listing Tiers & Direct Contact 2027 Rates

Standard Stallion Listing ($295 / year): Verified stallion profile, sire-line summary, standing farm placement, and regional search indexing.

Featured Stallion Placement ($695 / year): Priority homepage and leaderboard visibility, stud fee updates, and direct farm inquiry routing.

Full Farm Roster ($1,495 / year): Multi-stallion farm placement with priority regional visibility and roster updates.

About StallionRegistry.com

An independent international Thoroughbred stallion directory.

StallionRegistry.com is an independent directory covering Thoroughbred flat racing stallions, sire standings, and stud farms across Ireland, England, the United States (Kentucky, Florida and more), and Canada.

Our goal is to help broodmare owners, breeders, and bloodstock professionals find sound, proven sires and the farms that stand them, all in one place.

Listings are reviewed before they are published. Always confirm current stud fees and details directly with the stud farm before breeding.

To list a stallion or farm, use the Submit Listing page. For any other questions, email info@stallionregistry.com.
